1. System Architecture & Operational Flow

BRIDGES operates as a unified logistics deviation engine connecting real-time telemetry from GPS and IoT multi-sensors directly to operational decision-makers. When freight encounters route delays, thermal excursions, port gate congestions, or customs holds, the platform orchestrates collaborative recovery across every supply chain partner.

Lifecycle Phase Operational Activity Participating Personas & Data Artifacts
1. Ingestion & Detection Sub-second GPS telemetry and IoT thermal sensors broadcast position and cargo health. Automated SLA detection rules trigger exceptions. IoT Telematics Engine • System Store
Outputs automated severity flags (Critical, High, Medium).
2. Diagnostic & Triage Control tower triage queue categorizes incident root cause, evaluates temperature decay, and launches cross-functional incident War Rooms. Control Tower Analyst
Uses Investigation Workspace and War Room collaboration tools.
3. Planning & Intercept Dispatchers evaluate alternative routes, test detour miles, calculate fuel/toll overhead, and tender recovery loads to carriers. Dispatcher / Transport Planner
Employs Transport Planning Workbench and Secondary Carrier Tenders.
4. Fleet Execution Motor carriers review dispatched tenders, assign relief tractors and certified drivers, perform pre-trip checks, and confirm gate departure. Carrier Operations User
Acts within tenant-isolated Carrier Portal (`CAR-01`).
5. Hub Cross-Docking Warehouse teams schedule dock bays, inspect thermal probe readings, perform pallet transfers, and log security gate driver handovers. Warehouse Supervisor
Manages Dallas Central Hub (`WH-DAL-01`) dock doors & staging.
6. Stakeholder Advisory Customer care reviews approved arrival windows and uses AI Copilots to draft clear, non-blaming proactive delay notifications. Customer Service
Dispatches multi-channel alerts (SMS, Email, Portal).
7. Shipper Delivery & ePOD Shippers track cargo arrival via self-service portal. Carriers capture recipient digital signature and photo evidence of delivery. Customer / Shipper • Carrier Driver
Finalizes electronic Proof of Delivery (ePOD).

4. End-to-End Walkthrough Example: Cold-Chain Incident

The following walkthrough illustrates the complete operational lifecycle across all 7 roles when mitigating a high-severity cold-chain deviation:

Step 1: IoT Detection & Automated Alerting IoT Sensor • System Store

Shipment BRG-2843 (BioPharma Critical Cryo-Vaccine Consignment) is in transit from Dallas to Mountain Knot via refrigerated unit RT-04. The IoT sensor detects a temperature spike to -14.2°C, breaching the mandatory contract limit of -20.0°C. An automated Critical Exception is raised.

Step 2: Control Tower Triage & Forensics Control Tower Analyst (Sarah Lin)

Sarah Lin receives the Critical Alert on the Exception Queue (#/analyst_queue). In the Investigation Workspace (#/analyst_investigation), she inspects the telematics telemetry curve and detects a secondary refrigeration compressor stall. She launches a War Room session (#/analyst_war_room) and flags the incident for immediate cross-dock intercept.

Step 3: Route Re-planning & Carrier Recovery Tender Transport Dispatcher (Elena Rostova)

Elena Rostova opens the Planning & Routing Workspace (#/dispatcher_planning). She identifies an emergency rendezvous point at the Dallas Central Hub (`WH-DAL-01`), minimizing the added transit deviation to 1 hour 45 minutes. In Carrier Recovery Operations (#/dispatcher_recoveries), she issues Expedite Tender REC-2005 to partner CAR-01 (Bridges Global Logistics).

Step 4: Carrier Tender Acceptance & Driver Dispatch Carrier Operations (Marcus Vance)

Marcus Vance reviews the notification in Recovery Requests (#/carrier_requests) and accepts tender REC-2005. In Dispatch Execution (#/carrier_execution), he assigns relief refrigerated truck BG-12 and driver Norman Reedus, completing the pre-trip checklist and dispatching the vehicle to the Dallas hub.

Step 5: Warehouse Hub Intake & Quality Inspection Warehouse Supervisor (Carlos Mendoza)

At Dallas Hub (`WH-DAL-01`), Carlos Mendoza sees the emergency arrival on his Warehouse Dashboard (#/warehouse_dashboard). In Dock Scheduling (#/warehouse_docks), he assigns Bay 2. In Shipment Readiness (#/warehouse_readiness), his team performs a manual temperature probe check, verifies thermal stability, transfers the vaccine pallets to truck BG-12, and logs driver departure in Carrier Handover (#/warehouse_handover).

Step 6: Customer Care Advisory via AI Copilot Customer Service (Clara Hughes)

Clara Hughes spots the revised arrival window in her Customer Service Queue (#/cs_queue). In the CS AI Copilot (#/cs_ai_assistant), she generates a customer-safe message explaining a "routine equipment safety swap with cold-chain stability verified" confirming a revised delivery window of 2:30 PM. She sends the advisory via Compose Communication (#/cs_compose).

Step 7: Customer Self-Service Tracking & Final Delivery Customer • Carrier Driver

Sarah Jenkins opens her Customer Home (#/customer_home), reviews the notification, and monitors the final leg on Track Shipment (#/customer_track). Upon truck arrival at Mountain Knot, driver Norman Reedus captures her digital signature and photo in Delivery Confirmation (#/carrier_delivery). The electronic Proof of Delivery (ePOD) is instantly archived in both portals.

Step 8: Governance & Permanent Audit Record Administrator (Bima Ary)

Administrator Bima Ary reviews the completed incident in Exception History (#/exceptions). Carrier Bridges Global Logistics receives full SLA credit in Carrier Management (#/carriers) for meeting the expedited turnaround time. The complete sequence is permanently written to the immutable System Audit Log (#/audit).
